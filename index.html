The Eagles

The Philadelphia Eagles American football team is a staple of the City of Philadelphia. From wild fans to brutal rivalries, no other team can match the energy of their supporters like the Eagles do. Formerly known as the Frankford Yellow Jackets, they were founded in 1899 but weren't part of the NFL until 1924. Today they are a renowned team with two Super Bowl Championships and carry the pride of Philadelphia.
